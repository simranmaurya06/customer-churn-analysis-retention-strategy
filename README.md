# Customer Churn & Retention Analysis

## Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most critical challenges faced by telecommunication companies, as retaining existing customers is significantly more cost-effective than acquiring new ones.

This project analyzes customer churn behavior using Power BI to identify high-risk customer segments, understand key churn drivers, and provide actionable retention recommendations. The analysis focuses on customer contract types, tenure, internet services, and payment methods to uncover patterns associated with customer attrition.


## Business Problem

The organization is experiencing customer losses but lacks visibility into:

- Which customers are most likely to churn
- What factors contribute to customer attrition
- Which customer segments require immediate retention efforts
- What actions can reduce future churn

Without understanding these patterns, retention initiatives may be ineffective and lead to unnecessary revenue loss.


## Project Objectives

- Measure overall customer churn performance
- Identify customer segments with the highest churn risk
- Analyze the impact of contract type on customer retention
- Understand how customer tenure influences churn behavior
- Evaluate churn across internet service categories
- Examine the relationship between payment methods and churn
- Recommend data-driven retention strategies

## Key Findings

### Contract Type Analysis

- Month-to-Month customers exhibited the highest churn levels.
- Long-term contracts showed significantly lower churn.

### Customer Tenure Analysis

- Customers within the first 12 months had the highest churn rate.
- Churn decreased consistently as customer tenure increased.

### Internet Service Analysis

- Fiber Optic customers accounted for the highest number of churned customers.
- DSL customers showed comparatively lower churn levels.

### Payment Method Analysis

- Electronic Check users demonstrated the highest churn rate.
- Automatic payment methods were associated with lower churn rates.


## High-Risk Customer Profile

Customers most likely to churn typically exhibit the following characteristics:

- Month-to-Month Contract
- Customer Tenure below 12 Months
- Fiber Optic Internet Service
- Electronic Check Payment Method


## Recommended Retention Actions

- Encourage migration from Month-to-Month contracts to long-term plans.
- Strengthen onboarding and engagement programs for new customers.
- Investigate customer satisfaction issues related to Fiber Optic services.
- Promote automatic payment methods through incentives and awareness campaigns.
